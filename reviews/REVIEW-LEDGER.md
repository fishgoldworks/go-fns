### Applicability boundaries

This library does not implement equality/deep graph traversal, native type classification or conversion, parsing/deserialization, cryptography, authentication, runtime filesystem/network access or iterable-consuming collection algorithms. Their specialized checks are inapplicable unless inspection reveals a reachable use. Foreign/opaque values, sparse/inherited array-like inputs, coercion/getters, callback/proxy execution and denial-of-service limits remain relevant. Maintainer tooling does access files, processes and package registries and remains in the security/build scope.
